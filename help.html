Quick answers

The questions we hear most often

It says I’m not a member

You are almost certainly signed in to a different Patreon account from the one you pay with.

Log out of Patreon, then come back here, tap the round icon in the top-right corner and choose Sign in with the account that holds your membership.

Some games are locked

Games open by membership tier:

No accountClue Room
Transcript LibraryClue Room, Phrase Pairs
Fluency ClubAll five games

If you upgraded recently and a game is still locked, sign out and sign in again.

Where are the transcripts, PDFs and audio?

They are in each episode’s post on Patreon, ready to download for listening practice and shadowing.

How do I see my progress?

Tap the round icon in the top-right corner and choose Your progress. It shows your minutes, the phrases you have learned, and what to practise next.
